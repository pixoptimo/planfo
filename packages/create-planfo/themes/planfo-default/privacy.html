01 Overview & Commitment

Pixoptimo Labs ("Pixoptimo", "we", "us", or "our") develops and licenses premium, production-grade HTML5/CSS3 templates, UI components, and web design kits. This Privacy Policy outlines our procedures concerning the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of personal data when you visit our website, preview live interactive templates, or download our digital assets.

Our Core Privacy Principle: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers or ad networks. Our templates are pure vanilla web code with zero invasive analytics or telemetry dependencies.

By accessing or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please discontinue using our website.

02 Information We Collect

We adhere strictly to data minimization standards. The types of data we may collect include:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, and message content when contacting our support desk via email or WhatsApp.
  • Transaction Data: If you purchase commercial licenses, payment processing details (such as order ID and billing name) are handled securely by PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways. We never store credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: Aggregated, non-identifying server diagnostic data including browser type, operating system, timestamp, and referring URL to optimize server performance and detect malicious bot traffic.
  • Client-Side Preferences: Local storage flags, such as your chosen color scheme (pixoptimo_theme: dark or light mode), stored exclusively within your browser.

03 How We Use Your Information

We process personal data solely for legitimate business purposes and contract execution:

  • Fulfilling digital template delivery and license authentications.
  • Responding promptly to user support inquiries, customization requests, and technical assistance.
  • Maintaining and enhancing website uptime, responsiveness, and Core Web Vitals performance.
  • Preventing unauthorized redistribution, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Complying with statutory accounting and tax regulations.

04 Cookies & Local Storage

Unlike traditional template portals packed with third-party tracking cookies, Pixoptimo utilizes modern, non-invasive storage standards:

  • Essential Client Storage: We utilize browser localStorage solely to remember your UI theme preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode) across page transitions.
  • Zero Cross-Site Tracking: We do not place advertising cookies, behavioral fingerprinting scripts, or cross-domain tracking pixels.
  • User Control: You can clear your browser's local storage and cache at any time through your browser settings without affecting the core functionality of our template catalog.

05 Third-Party Integrations

We partner with trusted infrastructure partners to deliver rapid worldwide asset delivery:

  • Google Fonts: Typography assets (Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono) are served via privacy-friendly Google CDN endpoints.
  • Direct Communication Gateways: WhatsApp and Email links open directly inside your native client applications; no unsolicited messages or marketing lists are ever compiled.
  • Cloud Hosting & DDoS Protection: Edge networks verify request integrity to protect against volumetric cyberattacks.

06 Data Security & Retention

Pixoptimo enforces robust organizational and technological safeguards:

  • All web traffic is enforced using modern TLS 1.3 encryption with strict HTTPS headers.
  • Digital assets and code archives are hosted on isolated, encrypted cloud buckets with role-based access restrictions.
  • Customer communication logs are archived only as long as necessary to address your inquiry or satisfy statutory obligations (typically up to 12 months), after which they are permanently purged.

07 GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Regardless of your geographical location, we extend full global privacy rights:

  • Right of Access: You may request a summary of any personal data we hold associated with your email address.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request the total deletion of your contact records from our support archives.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We never deny service, adjust pricing, or degrade the template experience for users exercising their privacy rights.
