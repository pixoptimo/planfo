Frequently Asked Inquiries

Quick answers to common questions about our templates and CMS integration.

Can I use templates for commercial client projects?
Yes. All free and commercial templates include rights to build and deploy client websites with zero attribution required on client production domains.
How does Planfo CMS adapt my content?
Planfo CMS parses dynamic blocks, blueprints, and template metadata on publish, injecting formatted HTML directly into the static compilation pipeline without database overhead.
When will the Feature Hub and interactive forms launch?
The Feature Hub architecture is currently in development. Once completed, it will provide interactive voting, ticket tracking, and feature submissions within the dashboard.
Where do I report template rendering issues?
You can email our technical desk directly at pixoptimo@gmail.com with browser details or open an issue on our community GitHub repository.
